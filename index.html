Service before politics

Truth and Solidarity is a community movement contesting the 4 November 2026 Local Government Elections in Gauteng. We don’t believe you should have to wait for election posters to see what a movement stands for — so we put our work on a public ledger, where nothing can be hidden and nothing can be deleted.

See a leaking tap, a sewer spill, a broken road, illegal dumping, a blocked storm drain or a dead street light? Report it here. We fix what our volunteers can fix, escalate the rest to your municipality with the reference number published, and track every step in public until it’s done.

How it works

1
You report it — pin the spot, add a photo, 60 seconds. No account needed.
2
Everyone sees it — public map, public list, public reference number.
3
We act on it — fix it or escalate it, with every step time-stamped until the card turns green.

Our promise

  • 📬 We look at every report, same day.
  • 👷 What our volunteers can fix, we fix ourselves.
  • 🏛️ What needs the municipality, we escalate and publish the reference number so you can check we did.
  • 📖 Every status change is stamped on a public timeline. Nothing gets deleted.
  • 🔒 Your name and phone number are never shown publicly (POPIA).

🏆 Our flagship: Fix Ward 120

It started in Ward 120 — Lenasia South, Hospital Hills, Vlakfontein — inspired by communities like Roshnee that stopped waiting and started fixing. Now we’re opening the same public ledger to all of Gauteng.

Privacy

We collect only what you type in the report form. Your description, photo, area and location are public so the whole community can see the problem and its progress. Your name and phone number are stored securely, visible only to the Truth and Solidarity response team, used only to contact you about your report, and never shared or sold.
